About Game Paper.io 🗺️

Welcome to the neon grid of game paper.io. Your objective is simple: capture as much territory as possible. Draw closed lines to add sectors to your domain, but beware—if an enemy slices your line before it's closed, your game is over.

Mastering game paper.io requires strategic planning and quick reflexes. Play defensively to protect your borders, or aggressively to steal space from rivals. Dive in and dominate the leaderboards!
