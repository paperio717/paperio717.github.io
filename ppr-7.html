Experience the Original Paper.io 1 💧

Step back into the classic arena with paper.io 1! Before all the crazy updates and spin-offs, there was the pure, unadulterated joy of the original game. Capture blocks, build your square empire, and dominate the server.

Playing paper.io 1 is simple to learn but hard to master. Move your block around to draw a perimeter and return to your colored zone to capture the space. Don't let your enemies touch your tail, or it's game over! Enjoy this nostalgic trip to the clouds.
