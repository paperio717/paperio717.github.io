What is Paper IO? 🤔

Paper io is the incredibly addictive multiplayer game where you are a colored block trying to take over the map! Start small and draw lines to expand your territory. The bigger your space, the higher your score.

But watch out! In paper io, if another player crosses your line while you are drawing outside your safe zone, you pop! Outsmart your opponents, trap them, and become the undisputed ruler of the board.
